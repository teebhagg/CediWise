# Budget with clarity

See where your money goes and plan the month ahead. Built for cedis, MoMo, and real Ghana spending.

## What you get

- **Categories that make sense:** Food, transport, rent, MoMo, savings. Set a limit per category instead of guessing.
- **Spent vs left:** A simple bar shows what you have used and what is still available this month.
- **Safe to spend today:** The app tells you how much you can spend today without breaking the month.
- **Run-out date:** If you keep spending at this pace, you see roughly when money might finish.
- **Spending charts:** Bar, line, and donut views so you can spot what ate your salary.
- **Works offline:** Log expenses on the go. Sync when you are back online.

## What this is

A budget is a plan for your money. You decide how much goes to rent, food, transport, church, family support, savings, and everything else. Then you log what you actually spend. CediWise shows the gap between the plan and reality before you are surprised on the 25th.

## How it works

1. Start with your take-home pay. Use the salary calculator or type your net income. That is the pot you are working with this month.
2. Split it into categories: needs (rent, utilities, food), wants (eating out, data bundles), and savings. You can use the 50/30/20 idea as a starting point or set your own numbers.
3. Log spending as it happens. Each entry updates your progress bars.
4. Check safe-to-spend. The app answers "How much can I spend today?" and "When does the money run out at this pace?"

## Spending insights

Charts break down spending by category and by week or month. That is how you catch the small leaks: daily food, ride fares, subscriptions you forgot, repeated MoMo charges. It lives inside your budget: https://www.cediwise.app/spending-insights

## Who this helps

- Salaried workers who earn enough but still run dry before month end.
- People paid in cash and MoMo who never see a bank statement.
- Anyone tired of opening Notes or Excel every evening.
