# Learn while you grow

Practical financial education for everyday decisions. Written for Ghana, not copied from abroad.

## What you get

- **Short lessons:** Most take under five minutes.
- **Ghana-first topics:** MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, Treasury Bills, GSE, local banks, PAYE, SSNIT.
- **No finance degree:** Plain language. Terms are explained when they are used.
- **One small action:** Each lesson ends with something you can do today.
- **Growing library:** New lessons are added over time. Budgeting, saving, fraud, and investing basics.
- **Free:** All lessons are free in the app.

## What you will learn

- **Budgeting:** How to build a first monthly plan, track spending, and adapt the 50/30/20 rule for Ghanaian incomes.
- **Savings:** Emergency funds, bank savings versus Treasury Bills, and why a buffer matters before you invest.
- **Mobile money:** How MoMo works, typical fees, daily limits, and how to avoid paying more than you need to.
- **Fraud and safety:** Common scams, phishing, fake investment pitches, and how to protect your MoMo PIN.

## How it fits the app

Lessons connect to the tools. Read about PAYE, then run your salary through the calculator: https://www.cediwise.app/salary-calculator . Learn about VAT, then open the SME ledger: https://www.cediwise.app/sme-ledger

## Who this helps

- First-job earners who never got formal money education.
- Side hustlers who need VAT and record basics.
- Anyone who wants clear answers without paying for a seminar.
