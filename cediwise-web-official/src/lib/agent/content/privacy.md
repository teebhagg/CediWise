# Privacy Policy

Last updated July 5, 2026

CediWise runs salary calculators, budgets, SME ledgers, and lessons on web and mobile. This page explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

## 1. Introduction

By using CediWise you agree to this policy. If anything here is unclear, email joshua.ansah@cediwise.app

## 2. Controller and contact

Data controller: CediWise. For privacy inquiries, contact joshua.ansah@cediwise.app

## 3. What we collect

- Account and identity data: email address, phone number (used for one-time codes), name, and Google account identifiers for Google Sign-In.
- Financial and usage data: salary inputs, budgets, small and medium enterprise (SME) ledger entries, and transactions you enter (stored to provide the service).
- Device and technical data: device model, OS version, app version, IP address, and crash reports (for troubleshooting and analytics).
- Analytics and telemetry: usage patterns via Firebase Analytics to improve the app.
- Communications: emails you send, newsletter sign-ups, and support responses.
- Optional MoMo SMS import (mobile, opt-in): if you enable Mobile Money (MoMo) import, Android permissions let CediWise read or receive SMS on this device. CediWise filters for MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, and AT Money alerts on the device. Matching message text, sender, time, and parsed transaction details may be sent to CediWise's API hosted by Vercel and processed or stored by Supabase to identify, deduplicate, and add budget transactions. Import logs containing message text are retained for up to 180 days. Unrelated SMS are not sent. You may also paste a single MoMo SMS manually. On iOS, a similar import may use Apple Shortcuts you configure (optional, when available).

## 4. MoMo SMS auto-tracking (optional)

This feature is off by default and requires your explicit consent in the app.

- On the device: Android permissions allow access to SMS on this device. CediWise filters for MoMo alert senders and patterns before sending anything.
- Sent to CediWise: matching SMS body, sender ID, received time, and parsed fields (amount, counterparty, reference where available) may be sent to our Vercel-hosted API and processed or stored by Supabase to identify transactions, prevent duplicates, and create budget entries.
- Not sent: unrelated SMS, contacts, call logs, or continuous monitoring beyond MoMo-style filters you enable.
- Retention and processors: import logs containing SMS text are retained for up to 180 days. Vercel hosts the API, and Supabase processes and stores import data.
- Accuracy: parsing may miss or misclassify messages. Review imported entries and correct them when needed.
- Disable anytime: turn off tracking in Profile settings and revoke SMS permissions in your device settings. On iOS, also disable or delete the Message automation in the Shortcuts app, otherwise it can keep opening CediWise for matching messages.

## 5. Legal bases

- Performance of a contract: account and financial data.
- Legitimate interests: security, product improvement, and analytics.
- Consent: marketing emails and newsletters (opt out anytime).
- Consent: optional MoMo SMS import and related device permissions (withdraw by disabling the feature).

## 6. How we use your data

- Provide, operate, and maintain the Services.
- Authenticate you (one-time codes via SMS, Google Sign-In).
- Improve and personalize the Services (analytics).
- Communicate about your account, support, updates, or legal notices.
- Detect and prevent fraud and misuse.
- Parse optional MoMo SMS you provide (automatically or by paste) to suggest or create budget transactions.

## 7. Third parties and processors

We use trusted providers: Supabase (database and authentication), Firebase (analytics), Google (Sign-In), Arkesel (SMS one-time codes), and Vercel and GitHub (hosting). We share only the minimum data required. Refer to each provider's privacy policy for details.

## 8. Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Financial entries and user content are kept until you delete them or close your account, except where backups or legal holds apply.

MoMo SMS import logs: raw SMS text and import metadata used for troubleshooting and deduplication are kept for up to 180 days, then deleted. Budget transactions created from imports are kept like other transactions until you delete them or close your account.
