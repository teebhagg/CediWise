# Run your business smarter

Stay on top of expenses and cash flow. Sales, costs, VAT, and records in one place.

## What you get

- **Sales and expenses:** Record money in and money out. Tag by category so reports make sense later.
- **VAT handled for you:** 20% VAT calculated on eligible amounts. Less manual math, fewer mistakes.
- **GHS 750K alert:** A running turnover total with a heads-up before you cross VAT registration territory.
- **Monthly profit snapshot:** Revenue minus expenses for the month. See if the business actually made money.
- **CSV for your accountant:** Export records instead of handing over a notebook.
- **Side hustle friendly:** For traders, freelancers, and small shops. No accounting degree required.

VAT rate: 20%, applied automatically under VAT Act 1151.

## What this is

A simple business notebook inside your phone. You record sales (money coming in) and expenses (money going out). CediWise adds up totals, applies VAT where needed, and shows whether the month was profitable.

## How it works

1. Log a sale: amount, date, what you sold. Mark whether VAT applies.
2. Log an expense: stock, delivery, airtime, packaging, transport.
3. Check your dashboard: total sales, total costs, estimated profit, and VAT collected or owed where relevant.
4. Watch turnover. If you sell goods and approach GHS 750,000 in annual turnover, the app warns you. Service businesses often need VAT registration earlier; the ledger still keeps your records straight.

## VAT in plain language

VAT is a tax on sales. Registered businesses charge it, file returns, and pay GRA. Getting the math wrong or missing registration can mean penalties. The ledger does the 20% calculation on each entry.

## Why records matter

GRA can ask for proof of income and expenses. Digital records also help when you apply for a loan or need to show a partner how the business is doing.
