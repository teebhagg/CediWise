# See where the cedis went

Charts from your real transactions. Handy when you are broke by the 20th and cannot tell why.

## What you get

- **Bar, line, and donut charts:** Same data, different views.
- **Pick your time range:** Last week, month, six months, or year.
- **By category:** Food, transport, bills, subscriptions. See which slice grew.
- **Average spend:** Your typical week or month, with a line when you are above or below normal.
- **From real entries:** Charts use the expenses you logged in CediWise. No bank sync is needed to start.
- **Ghana categories:** Food, trotro, MoMo, church, family.

## What this is

Spending insights turn your expense list into pictures. Instead of scrolling through dozens of MoMo and cash entries, you see how much went to food, transport, data, rent, and the rest. It is part of the budgeting tool: https://www.cediwise.app/budgeting-tool

## How it works

Every time you log spending in CediWise, it feeds the charts. Open insights, choose a time range, and switch chart types. Bar charts compare periods, line charts show trends, and donut charts show each category's share.

## Example

You earn GHS 4,500 net and have GHS 200 left by the 18th. The donut chart shows transport at 28% and food at 35%. That is a specific answer, so you can trim trotro or eating out next month instead of guessing.

## Works well with

- The budgeting tool, to set limits after you see the pattern.
- The debt dashboard, to compare loan payments with daily spending: https://www.cediwise.app/debt-dashboard
