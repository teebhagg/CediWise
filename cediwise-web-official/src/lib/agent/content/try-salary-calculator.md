# Check your take-home pay

Type your gross monthly salary. We show SSNIT, PAYE, and what lands in your account.

3 free runs per day. The limit resets at midnight Ghana time.

Gross monthly salary is entered in GHS. The calculator uses the 2026 GRA PAYE and SSNIT rules.

- Official PAYE bands: https://gra.gov.gh/domestic-tax/tax-types/paye/ (Ghana Revenue Authority)
- Guide: https://www.cediwise.app/salary-calculator
- The app offers unlimited checks: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cediwise.app

Does your payslip match these numbers? Compare the result with your payslip. A difference is a question for HR or payroll, not proof of an error.
